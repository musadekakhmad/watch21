Raja Shivaji

Rating: 9

Release Date: 2026-05-01

Runtime: 195 min

Genres: Action, History, Drama

Director: Riteish Deshmukh

Cast: Riteish Deshmukh, Sanjay Dutt, Abhishek Bachchan, Mahesh Manjrekar, Sachin Khedekar, Genelia D'Souza, Vidya Balan, Bhagyashree
