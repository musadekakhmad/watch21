Rape of Love

Rating: 5.6

Release Date: 1978-01-10

Runtime: 115 min

Genres: Drama

Director: Yannick Bellon

Cast: Nathalie Nell, Alain Fourès, Michèle Simonnet, Pierre Arditi, Daniel Auteuil, Bernard Granger, Alain Marcel, Gilles Tamiz

Nicole, nurse in Grenoble, is raped one night by four men. Deeply scarred, emotionally and physically, she thinks she will never recover from the trauma. Following a friend's advice, she decides to file a lawsuit.
